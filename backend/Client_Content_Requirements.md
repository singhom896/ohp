# BRICS Artist Registry: Client Content Requirements

This checklist reflects the current public portal navigation, page content, visible records, and media modules. Please provide final, approved material or confirm that existing material is accurate and may be published. Do not treat sample names, counts, dates, or media records as verified content.

**Status key:** **Present / confirm** means something is already displayed or stored and needs client approval; **Replace sample** means the current item is demonstrative or generic; **Client supply required** means approved content is needed; **Partially present** means some material exists, but it is incomplete or needs validation.

## Client Handoff Checklist

- Approve or replace the fixed sample artist names, profile details, profile totals, news, announcements, and event cards.
- Verify all event dates and deadlines, including whether past events should be archived or removed.
- Confirm the official member/partner-country list and the country-specific Nodal Agency and eligibility information.
- Approve all existing BRICS-related copy and the five published legal/policy pages; provide revisions where needed.
- Confirm the nine existing NOS/scheme PDFs are unrelated or identify any that should remain; supply current BRICS Registry PDFs and translations.
- Supply or approve final banners, artist portraits, gallery photographs, videos, event media, credits, and usage permissions.
- Confirm public contact details, social URLs, and whether newsletter subscription is part of the service.
- Provide verified statistics with definitions and update dates.
